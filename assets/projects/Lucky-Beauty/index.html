About Lucky Beauty

Beauty, your way.

The brand Lucky Beauty is all about makeup products that serve as lucky charms you can always rely on, whether it’s because of the brilliant formulas or the energy they give. Lucky Beauty is a brand with clean and thoughtful formulations. Our products are vegan, cruelty-free, paraben-free, preservative-safe, and dermatologist-tested. These are products made to make you look good—when you look good, you feel good, which makes you love yourself more. Then, when you love yourself more, that’s when you feel lucky.
